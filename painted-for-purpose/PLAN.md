# PAINTED FOR PURPOSE (P4P)

## NGO Plan of Action

1\. Executive Summary

Painted For Purpose (P4P) is a student-led non-governmental organisation
designed to provide financial support to Pakistani flood relief
charities through a self-sustaining creative fundraising model.

The organisation will organise art-based sessions in which selected
participants customise plain white T-shirts using fabric paints. The
completed T-shirts will then be marketed and sold through the official
Painted For Purpose Instagram page and website.

A fixed proportion of the revenue generated from each session will be
donated to selected flood relief charities, while the remaining funds
will be reinvested into future sessions. This allows P4P to continue
operating and expanding without relying entirely on repeated external
funding.

The organisation also provides participants with an opportunity to
contribute to community service while developing their creative skills
and strengthening their university applications.

2\. Introduction

Painted For Purpose aims to combine creativity, community service, and
charitable fundraising into a sustainable NGO model.

The primary purpose of P4P is to assist victims of flooding in Pakistan
by providing financial contributions to established flood relief
charities. At the same time, the organisation gives students and other
selected participants the opportunity to participate in meaningful
community service activities.

P4P will operate through organised painting sessions in which
participants create unique designs on T-shirts. These products will
subsequently be sold, generating revenue that can be divided between
charitable donations and the continuation of the organisation's
activities.

3\. Core Functionality

The organisation's activities will follow a straightforward cycle:

1\. Plain white T-shirts will be purchased in bulk in different sizes,
with an initial batch of approximately 20 T-shirts.

2\. Fabric paints, brushes, and other necessary artistic materials will
be purchased according to the expected number of participants.

3\. Interested participants will register through a personalised P4P
website using a designated Google Form.

4\. Participants will be selected according to the organisation's
selection criteria.

5\. The selected participants will attend an organised session at an
open venue.

6\. Each participant will customise a T-shirt using the provided
artistic materials.

7\. Completed T-shirts will be collected and prepared for sale.

8\. The finished products will be marketed and sold through the P4P
Instagram page and website.

9\. Revenue generated from the sales will then be allocated according to
P4P's financial mechanism.

This system allows every session to contribute towards both charitable
funding and the continuation of future sessions.

4\. Participant Incentives

P4P will provide incentives to encourage students and other suitable
participants to take part in its activities.

Participants will receive:

- A selected charity-approved certificate.

- A certificate signed by the administration of the funded charities and
  a prominent community-service-related government official, subject to
  obtaining the relevant approval.

- Formally approved community-service hours.

These incentives are intended to recognise participants' contributions
while providing them with documented evidence of their community-service
involvement.

5\. Participant Selection Process

To maintain the artistic quality of the products, P4P will use a
selection process for participants.

Individuals interested in participating will complete the designated
Google Form and provide information regarding their creative ability and
previous artistic experience.

Based on the current plan, applicants without previous artistic
experience will not be selected. The strongest applicants will then be
chosen for the first session, with an initial target of approximately
10–20 participants.

The selection process is intended to ensure that the final products
maintain a suitable standard for commercial sale while keeping the
sessions manageable.

6\. Session Planning & Initial Investment

The first session will require an initial investment from the willing
founders of P4P.

This investment will cover the purchase of:

- Plain white T-shirts.

- Fabric paints.

- Paint brushes.

- Other necessary artistic supplies.

- Initial session-related expenses.

The purpose of the initial investment is to establish the first batch of
products and provide the organisation with the resources necessary to
conduct its first session.

Following the first session, the organisation intends to use revenue
generated from T-shirt sales to finance subsequent sessions, reducing
the need for repeated founder investment.

**7. Financial Mechanism**

Following the completion and sale of the products from each session, the
accumulated funds will be divided according to P4P's planned financial
model.

For the initial model:

**50% \> Flood Relief Charities**

Half of the accumulated funds will be donated to two selected flood
relief charities.

**50% \> Reinvestment**

The remaining half will be reinvested into the organisation to finance
the next session, including the purchase of additional T-shirts and
artistic supplies.

This creates a cycle in which each session contributes directly to
charitable causes while also generating the resources required for the
organisation to continue operating.

**8. Growth Strategy**

P4P will seek to expand its reach through a combination of digital
marketing, campus promotion, and collaborations.

**Social Media**

The organisation will use its Instagram page and website to:

- Display completed T-shirt designs.

- Promote upcoming sessions.

- Advertise products for sale.

- Demonstrate the organisation's charitable impact.

- Attract future participants and customers.

**Campus Marketing**

P4P will promote its activities within schools, colleges, and
universities to increase awareness and attract suitable participants.

**Influencer Collaboration**

Local influencers may be invited to attend sessions and promote the
organisation through their social-media platforms.

These strategies are intended to increase public awareness, strengthen
participation, and ultimately increase the number of products that can
be produced and sold.

**9. Expansion Strategy**

As P4P develops, the organisation intends to increase the scale of its
operations.

The expansion strategy will focus on:

- Increasing the number of T-shirts produced in each session.

- Expanding the organisation's public reach.

- Developing collaborations with external organisations.

- Increasing participant and customer engagement.

- Creating further opportunities for charitable funding.

Greater production capacity would allow P4P to generate more revenue
while maintaining its charitable contribution model.

**10. Profit & Reinvestment Strategy**

The organisation's long-term financial strategy is based on gradually
reducing the proportion of revenue required for reinvestment as P4P
becomes established.

The initial launch will require founder investment to establish the
organisation and finance the first session.

The first session is projected to generate approximately **PKR
30,000–34,000 in revenue**, based on the current plan.

As the organisation develops, the intended progression is:

| **Stage** | **Reinvestment Strategy**                               |
|-----------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Launch    | Initial founder investment                              |
| Session 1 | Establish the organisation and generate initial revenue |
| Session 2 | Approximately 75% reinvestment                          |
| Session 3 | Approximately 50% reinvestment                          |
| Session 4 | Approximately 25% reinvestment                          |

P4P also proposes increasing the T-shirt batch by approximately 10
shirts per session as the organisation grows.

The long-term objective is to create a model where P4P can continue
operating through its own generated revenue while maintaining its
charitable contributions.

generating approximately PKR 32,000 in revenue.

**12. Beneficiaries**

Painted For Purpose is designed to create benefits for three primary
groups:

**Flood Victims**

Flood relief charities will receive a fixed proportion of the funds
generated from each session, allowing P4P to contribute towards relief
efforts.

**Participants**

Participants will gain community-service experience, receive recognised
certificates and service hours, and have the opportunity to contribute
to a charitable initiative through their creative abilities.

**Founders**

The founders will gain experience in establishing and managing an NGO,
while the proposed financial model allows surplus revenue to provide
salaries once the organisation becomes sufficiently established.

**13. Long-Term Vision**

The long-term vision of Painted For Purpose is to develop a sustainable
charitable organisation that can continuously generate funding for flood
relief without depending entirely on external donations.

Through increased production, stronger marketing, campus outreach,
influencer collaborations, and partnerships, P4P aims to expand its
participant and customer base.

The organisation ultimately seeks to establish a cycle of:

**Creative Participation, Product Creation, Product Sales, Charitable
Donation + Reinvestment, Larger Sessions, Greater Impact and Revenue.**

This model allows the organisation's charitable impact to increase
alongside its growth.

**14. Expected Outcomes**

Through the implementation of this plan, P4P expects to achieve the
following:

- Provide recurring financial contributions to selected flood relief
  charities.

- Establish a sustainable fundraising model.

- Give participants meaningful community-service opportunities.

- Provide participants with recognised certificates and service hours.

- Develop a strong public and social-media presence.

- Increase the number of T-shirts produced and sold over time.

- Establish partnerships and collaborations with external organisations.

- Create opportunities for the founders to develop entrepreneurial and
  organisational experience.

- Build a strong addition to participants' university applications
  through genuine community involvement.

**15. Conclusion**

Painted For Purpose combines creative expression with charitable
fundraising to create a sustainable model for supporting flood relief
efforts in Pakistan.

By allowing participants to transform ordinary T-shirts into unique
products and subsequently converting their work into charitable funding,
P4P creates value for participants, customers, founders, and most
importantly, flood relief beneficiaries.

The organisation's emphasis on reinvestment, social-media growth, campus
marketing, collaboration, and increasing production capacity provides a
foundation for long-term expansion.

Ultimately, Painted For Purpose aims to demonstrate that community
service can be combined with creativity and sustainable entrepreneurship
to generate continued charitable impact.
